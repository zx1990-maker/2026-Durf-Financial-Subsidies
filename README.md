# Quantifying Financial Subsidies to Chinese SOEs: An Asset Return-Based Framework and Analysis

This project aims to quantify the true scale of financial subsidies granted to Chinese State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs) and identify their primary drivers. 

Traditional methods of measuring these subsidies often face omission and double-counting issues. Leveraging the novel asset return-based framework developed by Lucas, Garcia, and Solberg (2026), this study pivots to the asset side of the balance sheet to measure financial subsidies for Chinese SOEs compared to private-sector counterparts. After calculating the subsidy values using accounting data, we will compare our findings to previous estimates from the literature to evaluate the magnitude of traditional measurement errors. Finally, we will apply machine learning techniques to identify both the characteristics that best predict the allocation of these hidden subsidies and the firm-level drivers of the discrepancies between our novel estimates and those found in prior literature.
